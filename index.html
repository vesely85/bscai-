Why paper stopped winning

The old model

  • Retrospective — proof assembled after the fact
  • Sampled — a few inspections stood in for everything
  • Disputable — your word against the complaint
  • Slow — problems surfaced weeks later

What buyers expect now

  • Watch the work as it happens
  • Time-stamped, photo-evidenced audit trail
  • Live dashboards — in operations and in the bid room
  • Issues surfaced the same shift, not next month
The big shift: a building is now a stream of live data — foot traffic, lighting, HVAC, restroom dispensers — and every signal can call for service where it's needed, or tell you a space is fine to skip.

Proof: one app, whole workforce

The operating impact — measured across the workforce

When paper checklists become in-app validation, hours come back to the workforce. Cleaners stop re-handling and re-filing forms; supervisors stop rebuilding the day from clipboards, spreadsheets and phone calls to answer “was this done?”

And it holds up where the work happens — every scan, photo and timestamp is captured on the device even with no Wi-Fi or cellular, then syncs when signal returns. Nothing is lost, nothing is re-entered.

Proof: cleaning that answers to flights

A major Canadian airport — 10-year contract

The airport invited innovation on a decade-long industrial cleaning bid. The winning answer replaced static schedules with a demand engine: live flight data generates and assigns work as gates cycle through arrivals and departures.

Every gate cleaning captures pre/post photos and time-on-task. Dispatchers reassign work in real time as flight schedules shift.
